# Day 01 Assignment

## Overview

On Day 1 of the Coforge Training Program, we learned about the difference between **Software Products** and **Software Projects**.

A **Product** is developed based on market demand and can be used by many customers.

A **Project** is developed for a specific client based on their requirements.

In this assignment, we identified different examples of products and projects and understood how they are different from each other.

## What I Learned

- Difference between a Product and a Project.
- Real-world examples of software products and projects.
- Importance of client-specific and market-based software.
- Basic use of Git and GitHub to manage and upload assignments.
